How it works

From name to proof in three steps.

  1. Choose a design

    Round, double ring, scalloped, framed, or the “For Company” signature block.

  2. Add the details

    Company name and location. For the signature block, choose Director or Proprietor.

  3. Download the proof

    Save it as a PNG, JPG or PDF and send it to your customer to approve.

PROOF

Proofs, not production files.

Every export carries a PROOF mark so a customer can approve the layout before the stamp is made. Check the customer’s registration papers before you produce any seal.
